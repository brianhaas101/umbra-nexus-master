Layer 04 scores claim relevance.

It only uses explicit terms present in the sourced claim or source title.
It does not infer strategic meaning.
It does not create contacts.
It does not mutate the claim.
It does not modify Layer 01, 02, or 03 outputs.

Relevance inputs:
- claim text
- source title
- source authority score
- city-local classification

Relevance classes:
- 85–100: critical_operational_relevance
- 70–84: high_operational_relevance
- 45–69: moderate_operational_relevance
- 20–44: low_operational_relevance
- 0–19: not_operationally_relevant
