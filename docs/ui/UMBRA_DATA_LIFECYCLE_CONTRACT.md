\# UMBRA DATA LIFECYCLE CONTRACT



\# CORE PRINCIPLE



All operational data must progress through deterministic lifecycle stages with full provenance and replay visibility.



\---



\# LIFECYCLE STAGES



1\. Discovery

2\. Acquisition

3\. Validation

4\. Normalization

5\. Replay Verification

6\. Canonical Promotion

7\. Historical Preservation

8\. Archival



\---



\# IMMUTABILITY RULES



Source artifacts must remain immutable.



Canonical artifacts must remain replay-safe.



Historical records must remain auditable.



\---



\# FAILURE RULES



Lifecycle failures must expose:

\- provenance corruption

\- replay mismatch

\- topology failure

\- archival corruption

\- synchronization drift



Silent lifecycle mutation is prohibited.



\---



\# FINAL GUARANTEE



No operational artifact may bypass:

\- provenance preservation

\- replay verification

\- deterministic serialization

\- audit-safe history
