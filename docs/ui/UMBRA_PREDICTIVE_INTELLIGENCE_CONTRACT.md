\# UMBRA PREDICTIVE INTELLIGENCE CONTRACT



\# CORE PRINCIPLE



Predictive Intelligence forecasts potential future operational states using replay-safe, provenance-linked, explainable models.



Predictions must never be represented as canonical truth.



\---



\# PREDICTIVE DOMAINS



Predictive systems may include:

\- infrastructure forecasting

\- risk forecasting

\- population forecasting

\- anomaly forecasting

\- narrative forecasting

\- convergence forecasting

\- governance forecasting



\---



\# REQUIRED OUTPUT PROPERTIES



Predictive outputs must expose:

\- prediction ID

\- temporal horizon

\- confidence score

\- provenance chain

\- replay linkage

\- uncertainty metadata

\- source influence



\---



\# FORECASTING RULES



Predictive systems must support:

\- scenario comparison

\- branch-safe forecasting

\- confidence decay

\- uncertainty propagation

\- replay-safe reconstruction



Opaque forecasting logic is prohibited.



\---



\# TEMPORAL RULES



Predictive systems must support:

\- historical comparison

\- forecast replay

\- branch divergence

\- prediction drift analysis

\- convergence evolution



\---



\# VISUALIZATION RULES



Predictive visualization may include:

\- forecast overlays

\- uncertainty contours

\- probability gradients

\- predictive heatmaps

\- branch projections



Predictions must remain visually distinct from validated operational truth.



\---



\# FAILURE RULES



Predictive systems must expose:

\- weak confidence

\- unstable projections

\- stale inputs

\- replay mismatch

\- convergence instability



Silent predictive certainty inflation is prohibited.



\---



\# STORAGE REQUIREMENTS



Predictive systems must support:

\- deterministic serialization

\- replay-safe restoration

\- append-oriented forecast history

\- provenance-linked prediction lineage



\---



\# FINAL GUARANTEE



No predictive output may exist without:

\- provenance

\- replay compatibility

\- confidence visibility

\- uncertainty exposure
