\# UMBRA CANONICAL PROMOTION CONTRACT



\# CORE PRINCIPLE



Canonical promotion is the controlled elevation of validated artifacts into operational truth state.



\---



\# PROMOTION REQUIREMENTS



Artifacts must pass:

\- provenance validation

\- normalization validation

\- topology validation

\- replay verification

\- audit verification



before canonical promotion.



\---



\# REQUIRED PROMOTION DATA



Canonical promotion records must expose:

\- source lineage

\- validation history

\- replay verification status

\- topology status

\- SHA256 hashes

\- promotion timestamp



\---



\# FAILURE RULES



Promotion failures must expose:

\- replay mismatch

\- provenance gaps

\- topology corruption

\- normalization instability



Silent canonical promotion is prohibited.



\---



\# FINAL GUARANTEE



No operational truth artifact may enter canonical state without:

\- replay verification

\- provenance linkage

\- deterministic validation
