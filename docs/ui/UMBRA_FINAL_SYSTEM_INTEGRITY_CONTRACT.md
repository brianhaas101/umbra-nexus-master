\# UMBRA FINAL SYSTEM INTEGRITY CONTRACT

\- rendering speed

\- convenience

\- automation shortcuts

\- aesthetic preference

\- deterministic replay

\- provenance visibility

\- topology integrity

\- audit-safe mutation history

\- branch-safe simulation

\- synchronization integrity

\- silent mutation

\- provenance rewriting

\- replay alteration

\- opaque convergence logic

\- visible
