\# UMBRA MULTI-OPERATOR COORDINATION CONTRACT



\# CORE PRINCIPLE



Multiple operators may collaborate within shared replay-safe operational environments.



Collaboration must preserve deterministic integrity.



\---



\# COORDINATION REQUIREMENTS



Multi-operator systems must support:

\- shared workspaces

\- synchronized replay

\- collaborative simulation

\- shared convergence analysis

\- audit-safe coordination



\---



\# SYNCHRONIZATION RULES



Operator synchronization must preserve:

\- replay integrity

\- provenance visibility

\- branch lineage

\- operational consistency



\---



\# CONFLICT RULES



Conflicts must expose:

\- operator divergence

\- branch collision

\- synchronization drift

\- provenance mismatch



Silent operator overwrite is prohibited.



\---



\# AUDIT RULES



All collaborative actions must remain:

\- auditable

\- replayable

\- provenance-linked

\- historically reconstructable



\---



\# FINAL GUARANTEE



No collaborative operation may bypass:

\- replay validation

\- provenance visibility

\- audit-safe history

\- deterministic synchronization
