\# UMBRA NARRATIVE INTELLIGENCE CONTRACT



\# CORE PRINCIPLE



Narrative Intelligence models information propagation, narrative formation, influence dynamics, and communication ecosystems.



Narrative systems must remain explainable, provenance-linked, and replay-safe.



\---



\# NARRATIVE DOMAINS



Narrative systems may include:

\- news propagation

\- social amplification

\- institutional messaging

\- information cascades

\- influence networks

\- misinformation analysis

\- sentiment evolution



\---



\# REQUIRED ENTITY PROPERTIES



Narrative entities must expose:

\- canonical ID

\- temporal linkage

\- provenance chain

\- replay linkage

\- influence metadata

\- confidence metadata



\---



\# PROPAGATION RULES



Narrative systems must support:

\- origin tracing

\- amplification tracking

\- influence propagation

\- convergence linkage

\- temporal replay



Opaque influence attribution is prohibited.



\---



\# TEMPORAL RULES



Narrative systems must support:

\- narrative replay

\- amplification chronology

\- influence evolution

\- branch divergence

\- predictive propagation



\---



\# VISUALIZATION RULES



Narrative visualization may include:

\- propagation graphs

\- influence clusters

\- amplification heatmaps

\- narrative timelines

\- sentiment gradients



Predicted narrative propagation must remain visually distinct from validated propagation.



\---



\# FAILURE RULES



Narrative systems must expose:

\- provenance gaps

\- uncertain attribution

\- stale narratives

\- replay mismatch

\- influence instability



Silent narrative certainty inflation is prohibited.



\---



\# STORAGE REQUIREMENTS



Narrative systems must support:

\- deterministic serialization

\- replay-safe restoration

\- append-oriented propagation history

\- provenance-linked influence tracking



\---



\# FINAL GUARANTEE



No narrative output may exist without:

\- provenance

\- replay compatibility

\- confidence visibility

\- influence explainability
