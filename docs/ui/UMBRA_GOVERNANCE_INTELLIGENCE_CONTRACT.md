\# UMBRA GOVERNANCE INTELLIGENCE CONTRACT



\# CORE PRINCIPLE



Governance Intelligence models administrative authority, policy systems, jurisdictional structures, and operational governance behavior.



Governance systems must remain provenance-linked and replay-safe.



\---



\# GOVERNANCE DOMAINS



Governance systems may include:

\- municipal governance

\- state governance

\- federal governance

\- regulatory systems

\- zoning

\- policy frameworks

\- legal structures

\- emergency management



\---



\# REQUIRED ENTITY PROPERTIES



Governance entities must expose:

\- canonical ID

\- jurisdiction linkage

\- spatial linkage

\- provenance chain

\- replay linkage

\- authority scope



\---



\# JURISDICTION RULES



Governance systems must support:

\- overlapping jurisdictions

\- authority hierarchy

\- policy inheritance

\- boundary enforcement

\- governance conflicts



\---



\# TEMPORAL RULES



Governance systems must support:

\- ordinance timelines

\- policy changes

\- regulatory history

\- emergency declarations

\- governance replay



\---



\# VISUALIZATION RULES



Governance visualization may include:

\- jurisdiction overlays

\- authority boundaries

\- policy influence zones

\- governance conflict regions



Canonical governance boundaries must remain visually distinct from simulated governance overlays.



\---



\# FAILURE RULES



Governance systems must expose:

\- conflicting authority

\- stale policy

\- provenance gaps

\- replay mismatch

\- unresolved jurisdiction overlap



Silent governance ambiguity is prohibited.



\---



\# STORAGE REQUIREMENTS



Governance systems must support:

\- deterministic serialization

\- replay-safe restoration

\- immutable source artifacts

\- append-oriented policy history



\---



\# FINAL GUARANTEE



No governance entity may exist without:

\- provenance

\- jurisdiction linkage

\- replay compatibility

\- authority visibility
