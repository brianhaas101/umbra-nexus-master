\# UMBRA AUDIT AND COMPLIANCE CONTRACT



\# CORE PRINCIPLE



All operational actions must remain auditable, replayable, and historically reconstructable.



\---



\# AUDIT REQUIREMENTS



Audit systems must record:

\- ingestion actions

\- normalization actions

\- replay operations

\- simulation branching

\- convergence analysis

\- operator mutations

\- orchestration events



\---



\# IMMUTABILITY RULES



Audit history must remain:

\- append-only

\- replay-safe

\- provenance-linked

\- tamper-visible



\---



\# FAILURE RULES



Audit failures must expose:

\- missing history

\- replay corruption

\- provenance corruption

\- synchronization drift



Silent audit loss is prohibited.



\---



\# FINAL GUARANTEE



No operational mutation may occur without:

\- audit visibility

\- replay linkage

\- provenance preservation
