\# UMBRA IDENTITY INTELLIGENCE CONTRACT



\# CORE PRINCIPLE



Identity Intelligence governs canonical entity resolution, identity linkage, and cross-layer entity coherence.



Identity systems are foundational operational truth systems.



\---

Identity systems may include:

\- people

\- organizations

\- infrastructure entities

\- governance entities

\- geospatial entities

\- operational assets

\- narrative entities



\---



\# REQUIRED ENTITY PROPERTIES



Every identity entity must expose:

\- canonical ID

\- provenance chain

\- temporal linkage

\- spatial linkage

\- replay linkage

\- convergence linkage



\---



\# RESOLUTION RULES



Identity systems must support:

\- duplicate detection

\- canonical resolution

\- alias linkage

\- temporal identity evolution

\- cross-layer reconciliation



Opaque identity merging is prohibited.



\---



\# TEMPORAL RULES



Identity systems must support:

\- alias evolution

\- branch-safe restoration



\---



\# SPATIAL RULES

\- canonical geometry

\- movement history



\---



\# FAILURE RULES



Identity systems must expose:

\- unresolved conflicts

\- duplicate ambiguity

\- stale identities

\- provenance gaps

\- replay mismatch

\---

\- deterministic serialization

\- canonical identity

\- provenance linkage
