\# UMBRA INFRASTRUCTURE\_INTELLIGENCE\_CONTRACT



\# CORE PRINCIPLE



Infrastructure Intelligence models physical systems, operational networks, utilities, logistics, and structural dependencies.



Infrastructure systems must remain geospatially anchored and replay-safe.



\---



\# INFRASTRUCTURE DOMAINS



Infrastructure systems may include:

\- transportation

\- utilities

\- energy

\- telecommunications

\- water systems

\- logistics

\- facilities

\- critical infrastructure



\---



\# REQUIRED ENTITY PROPERTIES



Infrastructure entities must expose:

\- canonical ID

\- spatial geometry

\- operational status

\- provenance chain

\- replay linkage

\- dependency graph



\---



\# DEPENDENCY RULES



Infrastructure systems must support:

\- upstream dependencies

\- downstream dependencies

\- failure propagation

\- network tracing

\- convergence integration



\---



\# TEMPORAL RULES



Infrastructure systems must support:

\- operational history

\- outage replay

\- maintenance chronology

\- simulation branching

\- future forecasting



\---



\# VISUALIZATION RULES



Infrastructure visualization may include:

\- network graphs

\- route overlays

\- utilization heatmaps

\- failure propagation

\- dependency chains



Canonical infrastructure must remain visually distinct from simulated infrastructure.



\---



\# FAILURE RULES



Infrastructure systems must expose:

\- outages

\- degraded confidence

\- stale telemetry

\- topology mismatch

\- dependency instability



Silent infrastructure failure masking is prohibited.



\---



\# STORAGE REQUIREMENTS



Infrastructure systems must support:

\- deterministic serialization

\- replay-safe restoration

\- immutable source artifacts

\- append-oriented operational history



\---



\# FINAL GUARANTEE



No infrastructure entity may exist without:

\- provenance

\- spatial linkage

\- replay compatibility

\- dependency visibility
