\# UMBRA PROVENANCE VISUALIZATION CONTRACT



\# CORE PRINCIPLE



Provenance visibility is mandatory operational context.



Provenance must remain accessible from every operational entity.



\---



\# REQUIRED VISUALIZATION DATA



Provenance systems must expose:

\- source origin

\- acquisition history

\- SHA256 hashes

\- normalization lineage

\- topology validation

\- replay validation

\- confidence metadata



\---



\# VISIBILITY RULES



Provenance must never be:

\- hidden behind deep navigation

\- detached from entities

\- visually ambiguous



\---



\# VISUAL RULES



Provenance indicators may include:

\- badges

\- outlines

\- opacity gradients

\- confidence overlays

\- lineage inspectors



\---



\# FAILURE RULES



Provenance failures must expose:

\- missing lineage

\- replay mismatch

\- stale acquisition

\- validation failure



Silent provenance degradation is prohibited.



\---



\# FINAL GUARANTEE



No operational entity may render without provenance visibility.
