\# UMBRA DIAGNOSTICS AND FAILURE CONTRACT



\# CORE PRINCIPLE



Operational failures must remain visible, explainable, and replayable.



Silent degradation is prohibited.



\---



\# FAILURE TYPES



Failures may include:

\- ingestion failure

\- replay divergence

\- topology mismatch

\- provenance corruption

\- synchronization drift

\- convergence instability

\- simulation corruption



\---



\# DIAGNOSTIC REQUIREMENTS



Diagnostics must expose:

\- failure origin

\- affected systems

\- replay impact

\- provenance impact

\- recovery status

\- confidence degradation



\---



\# VISIBILITY RULES



Critical failures must never be hidden behind:

\- modal stacks

\- secondary screens

\- collapsed panels



\---



\# RECOVERY RULES



Recovery systems must support:

\- deterministic rollback

\- replay reconstruction

\- branch-safe recovery

\- audit-safe restoration



\---



\# FINAL GUARANTEE



Every operational failure must remain:

\- visible

\- explainable

\- replayable

\- auditable
