\# UMBRA CONVERGENCE INTELLIGENCE CONTRACT



\# CORE PRINCIPLE



Convergence Intelligence synthesizes signals across all intelligence layers into explainable operational understanding.



Convergence must never behave as a black-box scoring engine.



\---



\# CONVERGENCE INPUTS



Convergence systems may consume:

\- spatial intelligence

\- temporal intelligence

\- infrastructure intelligence

\- governance intelligence

\- behavioral intelligence

\- narrative intelligence

\- predictive intelligence

\- simulation outputs



\---



\# REQUIRED OUTPUTS



Convergence outputs must expose:

\- confidence score

\- signal overlap

\- source weighting

\- provenance linkage

\- uncertainty range

\- replay linkage

\- anomaly indicators



\---



\# EXPLAINABILITY RULES



Every convergence output must expose:

\- why the score exists

\- which layers contributed

\- weighting methodology

\- confidence derivation

\- uncertainty propagation



Opaque convergence scoring is prohibited.



\---



\# VISUALIZATION RULES



Convergence may render:

\- heatmaps

\- confidence contours

\- anomaly regions

\- signal intersections

\- influence networks



Canonical truth must remain visually distinct.



\---



\# FAILURE RULES



Convergence systems must expose:

\- weak confidence

\- missing sources

\- stale inputs

\- replay mismatch

\- contradictory signals

\- provenance gaps



Silent confidence inflation is prohibited.



\---



\# REPLAY RULES



Convergence outputs must support:

\- deterministic replay

\- historical reconstruction

\- branch-safe restoration

\- simulation comparison



\---



\# STORAGE REQUIREMENTS



Convergence systems must support:

\- append-oriented history

\- replay-safe serialization

\- provenance-linked scoring

\- deterministic restoration



\---



\# FINAL GUARANTEE



No convergence output may exist without:

\- provenance

\- explainability

\- replay compatibility

\- confidence derivation
