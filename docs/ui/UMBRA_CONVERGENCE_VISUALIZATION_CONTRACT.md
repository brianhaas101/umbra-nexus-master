\# UMBRA CONVERGENCE VISUALIZATION CONTRACT



\# CORE PRINCIPLE



Convergence visualization must remain explainable, replay-safe, and provenance-linked.



\---



\# REQUIRED VISUALIZATION DATA



Convergence visualization must expose:

\- signal overlap

\- confidence scoring

\- uncertainty propagation

\- source weighting

\- anomaly indicators



\---



\# VISUALIZATION RULES



Convergence may render:

\- heatmaps

\- influence contours

\- overlap regions

\- anomaly clusters

\- weighted overlays



Canonical truth must remain visually dominant.



\---



\# FAILURE RULES



Convergence failures must expose:

\- unstable scoring

\- provenance gaps

\- stale inputs

\- replay mismatch



Silent convergence distortion is prohibited.



\---



\# FINAL GUARANTEE



No convergence rendering may exist without:

\- explainability

\- provenance

\- replay compatibility
