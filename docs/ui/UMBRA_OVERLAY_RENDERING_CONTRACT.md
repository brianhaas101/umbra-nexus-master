\# UMBRA OVERLAY RENDERING CONTRACT



\# CORE PRINCIPLE



Overlays augment operational understanding without obscuring canonical truth.



\---



\# OVERLAY TYPES



Supported overlays may include:

\- convergence overlays

\- risk overlays

\- simulation overlays

\- predictive overlays

\- infrastructure overlays

\- behavioral overlays



\---



\# RENDERING RULES



Overlays must remain:

\- replay-safe

\- provenance-linked

\- temporally synchronized

\- visually explainable



\---



\# VISUAL DIFFERENTIATION RULES



Canonical truth must remain visually distinct from:

\- predictions

\- simulations

\- inferred overlays

\- experimental overlays



\---



\# FAILURE RULES



Overlay failures must expose:

\- stale rendering

\- replay mismatch

\- provenance gaps

\- synchronization drift



Silent overlay degradation is prohibited.



\---



\# FINAL GUARANTEE



No overlay may render without:

\- provenance visibility

\- replay compatibility

\- synchronization integrity
