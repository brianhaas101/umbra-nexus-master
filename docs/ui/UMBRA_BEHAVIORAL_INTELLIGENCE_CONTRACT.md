\# UMBRA BEHAVIORAL INTELLIGENCE CONTRACT



\# CORE PRINCIPLE



Behavioral Intelligence models human, organizational, and systemic behavior patterns across operational environments.



Behavioral systems must remain explainable and replay-safe.



\---



\# BEHAVIORAL DOMAINS



Behavioral systems may include:

\- mobility behavior

\- population response

\- organizational behavior

\- infrastructure usage

\- crowd dynamics

\- migration patterns

\- emergency response behavior



\---



\# REQUIRED ENTITY PROPERTIES



Behavioral entities must expose:

\- canonical ID

\- spatial linkage

\- temporal linkage

\- provenance chain

\- replay linkage

\- confidence metadata



\---



\# MODELING RULES



Behavioral systems must support:

\- pattern recognition

\- anomaly detection

\- trend analysis

\- behavioral forecasting

\- simulation branching



Opaque behavioral inference is prohibited.



\---



\# TEMPORAL RULES



Behavioral systems must support:

\- historical playback

\- event correlation

\- behavioral replay

\- predictive progression

\- branch divergence



\---



\# VISUALIZATION RULES



Behavioral visualization may include:

\- movement flows

\- density overlays

\- trend gradients

\- anomaly clusters

\- response propagation



Behavioral predictions must remain visually distinct from validated behavioral observations.



\---



\# FAILURE RULES



Behavioral systems must expose:

\- weak confidence

\- sparse sampling

\- stale observations

\- replay mismatch

\- contradictory patterns



Silent behavioral overconfidence is prohibited.



\---



\# STORAGE REQUIREMENTS



Behavioral systems must support:

\- deterministic serialization

\- replay-safe restoration

\- append-oriented observation history

\- provenance-linked analysis



\---



\# FINAL GUARANTEE



No behavioral output may exist without:

\- provenance

\- confidence visibility

\- replay compatibility

\- uncertainty exposure
