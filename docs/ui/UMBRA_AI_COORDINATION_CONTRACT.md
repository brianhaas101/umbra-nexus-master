\# UMBRA AI COORDINATION CONTRACT



\# CORE PRINCIPLE



AI systems assist operational analysis but never override deterministic operational truth.



AI outputs must remain explainable and provenance-linked.



\---



\# AI RESPONSIBILITIES



AI systems may assist:

\- anomaly detection

\- convergence analysis

\- simulation support

\- ingestion assistance

\- operational summarization

\- workflow acceleration



\---



\# PROHIBITED AI ACTIONS



AI systems may never:

\- mutate canonical truth

\- rewrite provenance

\- bypass replay validation

\- fabricate operational state

\- silently alter geometry



\---



\# EXPLAINABILITY RULES



All AI outputs must expose:

\- source inputs

\- confidence metadata

\- uncertainty

\- provenance linkage

\- replay compatibility



\---



\# FINAL GUARANTEE



No AI-generated operational state may bypass:

\- provenance verification

\- replay validation

\- audit visibility
